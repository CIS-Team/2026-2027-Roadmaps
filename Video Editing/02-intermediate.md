# Weeks 4–6 · Sound Effects & Short-Form Editing

These weeks preserve the original tasks and tutorials in their original order.

## Week 4: Sound Effects Practice Task

The fourth task is available on the course website. The task has a four-day completion window.

### What you need to do
Use the video provided with the task and add suitable sound effects, such as a camera sound, wind, a whoosh, or a riser. Choose effects that fit the action and timing of the footage.

### Submission checklist
- [ ] Add sound effects that match the visuals.
- [ ] Adjust timing and volume so the effects feel intentional.
- [ ] Review the result from beginning to end.
- [ ] Submit the completed video before the four-day deadline.
