# 02 · Intermediate — Weeks 4–6

**Goal:** Edit with intention, build stronger pacing, and use sound design to make short-form videos more engaging.

**Recommended pace:** 6–8 hours per week.

---

## Week 4 · Storytelling and Reference Recreation

**Goal:** Understand how a finished video is structured and recreate its editing decisions.

### Topics
- Defining the purpose, audience and message of a video.
- Building a simple beginning, middle and ending.
- Shot selection, continuity and visual rhythm.
- Analyzing a reference video shot by shot.
- Planning cuts, titles, transitions and timing before editing.
- Recreating a style while using footage and assets you are allowed to use.

### Practice
1. Choose a short reference reel (15–45 seconds).
2. Break it down into shots and note each cut's approximate timing.
3. Identify titles, transitions, speed changes and audio cues.
4. Recreate the structure with your own footage or permitted assets.
5. Compare your result with the reference and list three improvements.

### Weekly task · Reference recreation
Submit the reference link, a short breakdown, your exported recreation and the editable project.

### Exit checklist
- [ ] I can break a short video into shots and editing decisions.
- [ ] I can explain why a cut or transition is used.
- [ ] I can recreate timing without blindly copying effects.
- [ ] I can identify what should be improved after comparing versions.

---

## Week 5 · Sound Design and Audio Mixing

**Goal:** Use audio layers to support the visuals and create a more immersive edit.

### Topics
- Dialogue, music, ambience and sound effects.
- Finding and organizing suitable sound effects.
- Whoosh, riser, impact, wind and camera sound effects.
- Synchronizing sound effects with movement and cuts.
- Audio fades, keyframes and volume automation.
- Basic EQ, noise reduction and loudness awareness.
- Avoiding overcrowded mixes and protecting dialogue clarity.

### Practice
1. Start with a short video that has little or no sound design.
2. Add suitable ambience and selected sound effects.
3. Synchronize effects with visible actions and transitions.
4. Add music only if it supports the mood.
5. Adjust levels and listen on headphones and speakers.

### Weekly task · Sound-designed edit
Submit the original silent/rough version and the final version with sound design. Include the project file and list the audio sources and licenses.

### Exit checklist
- [ ] I can distinguish dialogue, ambience, music and effects.
- [ ] I can synchronize sound effects with visual events.
- [ ] I can create smooth audio fades.
- [ ] I can keep audio clear without clipping.
- [ ] I can choose effects that support rather than distract from the story.

---

## Week 6 · Short-form Editing Workflow

**Goal:** Produce a polished vertical reel using a repeatable editing workflow.

### Topics
- Vertical formats such as 9:16 and platform-safe framing.
- Hook, pacing, visual variety and retention.
- Captions and readable on-screen text.
- Speed changes and simple masking where appropriate.
- Consistent typography and visual style.
- Reviewing edits for dead time, awkward cuts and distracting effects.
- Exporting and checking a social-media-ready file.

### Practice
1. Plan a 20–45 second vertical video.
2. Create a strong opening hook.
3. Edit the main sequence with intentional pacing.
4. Add captions or short on-screen text.
5. Use sound design and transitions only where they add value.
6. Export and review the video on a phone-sized screen.

### Weekly task · Polished reel
Create a **20–45 second vertical reel**. Submit the final MP4, editable project, and a short note explaining your editing choices.

### Exit checklist
- [ ] I can set up a 9:16 sequence.
- [ ] I can create a clear hook and maintain pacing.
- [ ] I can make captions readable on a small screen.
- [ ] I can review and refine an edit before delivery.
- [ ] I can package the project and its assets.

**Milestone 2:** A polished short-form video with intentional sound design.
