## About this roadmap

A practical, project-based path for beginners who want to learn video editing and build a foundation in motion graphics. It starts with **Adobe Premiere Pro**, moves through storytelling and sound design, and finishes with **Adobe After Effects**.

You do not need prior editing experience. A computer capable of running the chosen software, sample footage, and consistent practice are enough to begin.

> **The golden rule:** Do not just watch tutorials. Recreate the technique, then use it in a different edit of your own.

## How to study

Suggested weekly commitment: **6–8 hours**.

| Session | Activity | Suggested time |
| :-- | :-- | :--: |
| 1 | Watch the main lesson and take notes | 1.5 h |
| 2 | Follow along and recreate the demonstrated techniques | 1.5–2 h |
| 3 | Practice without the tutorial | 1 h |
| 4 | Complete the weekly task | 2 h |
| 5 | Review, export, organize files and update the tracker | 1 h |

### Learning rules

- Use keyboard shortcuts gradually; learn the shortcuts you use most.
- Keep footage, audio, graphics and project files organized.
- Watch with a purpose: pause, recreate, and experiment.
- Use music and sound effects only when you have permission to use them.
- Save versions before major changes and keep project files with their assets.
- Do not move to the next week until you can complete the task without copying every step.

## Milestone projects

1. **First edit (Week 3):** a 30–60 second video with clean cuts, titles, basic color correction and balanced audio.
2. **Short-form reel (Week 6):** recreate a reference reel, then make an original version using your own footage and sound design.
3. **Final motion piece (Week 9):** a 30–60 second edit combining Premiere Pro editing with a short After Effects title or motion-graphics sequence.
